# Brief (from Pedro's call with Jamie, 2026-11-06)

Jira: VLABS-277 (under the BidSidekick epic VLABS-264).

## Jamie's work

Jamie does graphic design for landscape plans: she redraws the plans she receives (PDF, sometimes
JPEG) as presentation drawings in Adobe Illustrator for Shane, a landscape design-and-build business.

1. **Reading the plan.** She pulls out plant notations, street names, dimensions (when they matter to
   the diagram) and other markings, and retypes them onto the new drawing.
2. **Plant graphics.** She matches each plant on the plan to a graphic in her own library.
3. **Title block** (bottom or side panel). It holds the scale, client name, job address, a disclaimer
   (usually "not for construction"), a copyright statement (Shane will supply one) and Shane's logo.
4. **Export.** She saves and downsamples using Illustrator Actions. This is out of scope.
5. **Invoicing Shane.** Each job needs a job number, and she invoices per job.

## Pricing

Not hourly: childminding interruptions make time hard to track, and fast, experienced work shouldn't
earn less. Instead she charges fixed prices by job size (land size and scope decide how many elements
she draws). She will time a few practice jobs to set the prices.
